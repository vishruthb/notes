Discrete Mathematics provides tools for counting, arranging, and analyzing finite sets:
- [[📊 Permutations]] deal with ordering elements when sequence matters.
- [[🔢 Combinations]] deal with selecting subsets where order doesn’t matter.
- [[⚖️ Inclusion-Exclusion]] allows us to count with overlaps by alternating adds and subtracts.
- [[⭐ Stars and Bars]] allow us to distribute indistinguishable items into bins.
- [[☝️ Integer Equations]] help us solve equations over nonnegative integers.
- [[📚 Binomial Coefficients]] allow us to choose combinations.
- [[🐇 Fibonacci Numbers]] are a recursive sequence with combinatorial interpretations.
