discrete mathematics provides tools for counting, arranging, and analyzing finite sets:
- [[📊 Permutations|📊 permutations]] deal with ordering elements when sequence matters.
- [[🔢 Combinations|🔢 combinations]] deal with selecting subsets where order doesn’t matter.
- [[⚖️ Inclusion-Exclusion|⚖️ inclusion-exclusion]] allows us to count with overlaps by alternating adds and subtracts.
- [[⭐ Stars and Bars|⭐ stars and bars]] allow us to distribute indistinguishable items into bins.
- [[☝️ Integer Equations|☝️ integer equations]] help us solve equations over nonnegative integers.
- [[📚 Binomial Coefficients|📚 binomial coefficients]] allow us to choose combinations.
- [[🐇 Fibonacci Numbers|🐇 fibonacci numbers]] are a recursive sequence with combinatorial interpretations.
