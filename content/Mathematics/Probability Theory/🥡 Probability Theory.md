probability theory models and quantifies uncertainty in events:
- [[🧮 Bernoulli and Binomial Distributions|🧮 bernoulli and binomial distributions]] measure the probability of k successes in n independent trials.
- [[🔄 Uniform Distributions|🔄 uniform distributions]] assign equal probability to each outcome in a finite sample space.
- [[🎲 Random Variables|🎲 random variables]] map experiment outcomes to numerical values for analysis.
- [[☁️ Conditional Probability|☁️ conditional probability]] measures the probability of an event given that another event has occurred.
- [[🎯 Bayes' Theorem|🎯 bayes' theorem]] updates the probability of a hypothesis based on new evidence.
